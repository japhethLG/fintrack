# Cluster C3

occurrenceCalculator date-generation edge cases (semi-monthly dup, daily weekend collapse, weekend leaks outside window, month-end clamp)

Source files implicated:
- app/lib/logic/projectionEngine/occurrenceCalculator.ts

## Member findings (from first-round analysts)

### [F3] (high/edge-case) Semi-monthly + weekend adjustment can place two paydays on the same calendar day, double-counting cash flow
- File: app/lib/logic/projectionEngine/occurrenceCalculator.ts : 115-123
- Why wrong: Each specificDay is independently weekend-adjusted with no de-duplication. When two configured days are close (e.g. [14,15], or [15,16]) and a weekend pushes both onto the same business day, two occurrences land on the identical scheduledDate. There is no collision detection or merge, so the day shows two separate payments for what should be a single logical schedule overlap, and the daily/forecast balance is computed off the doubled amount. (Combined with the occurrenceId issue above, the two may even share an ID.)
- Scenario: frequency 'semi-monthly', specificDays [14,15], weekendAdjustment 'after', Feb 2026 (the 14th is Saturday, 15th is Sunday). Verified via node: day14 -> Mon 2026-02-16; day15 -> Mon 2026-02-16. Two paychecks land on 2026-02-16. Correct behavior: the engine should either merge same-day occurrences or stagger them; the calendar should not show two duplicate semi-monthly payments on one day.
- Suggested fix: After generating the month's semi-monthly dates, de-duplicate by adjusted scheduledDate (or shift a colliding payday to the next/previous business day) before pushing occurrences.
- First-round verification: 3 votes, 0 refutes

### [F4] (high/edge-case) Monthly/quarterly/yearly dayOfMonth clamping makes month-end occurrences non-stable and silently merges short-month paydays
- File: app/lib/logic/projectionEngine/occurrenceCalculator.ts : 130-148
- Why wrong: clampDayToMonth pins dayOfMonth 31 to Feb 28/29, Apr 30, etc. The occurrence-date logic itself is acceptable (verified: Jan31, Feb28, Mar31, Apr30...). The latent correctness problem is the interaction with the occurrenceId for monthly being `src_YYYY-MM` (occurrenceIdGenerator.ts line 86-87) which is stable, but for the START-DATE comparison and weekend adjustment a clamped Feb-28 date plus weekendAdjustment can push into March, while the next true March occurrence (Mar 31) also exists — these are different IDs (different months) so they survive, but a weekendAdjustment='after' on a month-end date crosses into the following month, mislabeling the calendar period. Specifically a 'dayOfMonth 31' payment that is Sunday May 31 with adjustment 'after' is moved to Jun 1, so the 'May' bill (occurrenceId src_YYYY-05) is shown in June, distorting the June daily balance and the monthly income/expense totals for both months.
- Scenario: frequency 'monthly', dayOfMonth 31, weekendAdjustment 'after'. May 31 2026 is a Sunday. Verified via node: raw May 31 2026 -> adjusted Mon Jun 01 2026 (MONTH=6). The May bill appears on Jun 1, so May's monthly total is missing it and June's total includes an extra month-end bill, even though its occurrenceId is 'src_2026-05'. Correct behavior per spec ('pay Friday if weekend' / 'pay Monday'): for month-end paydays the adjustment should not cross into a different month/period (typically push 'before' to stay in-month, or keep period attribution by month).
- Suggested fix: Clamp the weekend-adjusted date back into its logical month/period, or attribute the daily-balance/period totals by occurrenceId (logical period) rather than by the shifted scheduledDate; at minimum avoid 'after' crossing a month boundary for month-end occurrences.
- First-round verification: 3 votes, 1 refutes

### [F39] (high/edge-case) Semi-monthly: two specificDays that clamp to the same day-of-month produce duplicate occurrences and a colliding ID
- File: app/lib/logic/projectionEngine/occurrenceCalculator.ts : 105-128
- Why wrong: clampDayToMonth caps each specificDay to the month length. In short months two distinct configured days can clamp to the SAME calendar day, producing two occurrences on the identical date. No de-duplication is performed. Additionally getSemiMonthlyIndex in occurrenceIdGenerator.ts assigns the SAME index to both (both fall into the day<=sorted[0] / >sorted[0] bucket), so the two occurrences also share one occurrenceId. The engine should collapse specificDays that clamp to the same day (e.g. de-dupe the resolved actualDay values per month).
- Scenario: semi-monthly income, specificDays=[29,30], February 2025. Day 29 -> clampDayToMonth=28; Day 30 -> clampDayToMonth=28. The engine pushes TWO occurrences both on 2025-02-28, doubling that paycheck/bill for February. getSemiMonthlyIndex(28,[29,30]) returns 1 for both, so both get occurrenceId `${id}_2025-02-1`. Correct behavior: exactly one occurrence on Feb 28 for that month (the two configured days coincide in February). [30,31] and [29,31] in February exhibit the same double-count.
- Suggested fix: Within the per-month loop, compute the set of distinct clamped days (e.g. Array.from(new Set(specificDays.map(d => clampDayToMonth(d, year, month))))) before creating dates, so colliding days emit a single occurrence; and make getSemiMonthlyIndex robust to clamped collisions.
- First-round verification: 3 votes, 0 refutes

### [F40] (medium/correctness) Weekend adjustment is applied after the range check, so occurrences leak outside [viewStart, effectiveEnd] and boundary occurrences are dropped
- File: app/lib/logic/projectionEngine/occurrenceCalculator.ts : 120-122 (semi-monthly), 141-142 (monthly), 161-162 (quarterly), 179-180 (yearly), 44-45 (one-time)
- Why wrong: The window membership test (date >= viewStartDate && date <= effectiveEnd) is evaluated on the RAW occurrence date, but the value pushed is adjustForWeekend(date), which can move the date by up to +2 days (Sat 'after' -> Mon) or -2 days (Sun 'before' -> Fri). Therefore: (a) an occurrence whose raw date is inside the window but whose adjusted date is outside leaks past the boundary; and (b) an occurrence whose raw date is just outside the window but whose adjusted date falls inside is never generated (missing transaction at the edge of a month/calendar view).
- Scenario: Monthly bill on the 1st, weekendAdjustment='after', calendar view for March 2025 (viewStart=Mar 1, effectiveEnd=Mar 31). Mar 1 2025 is Saturday; raw Mar 1 passes the range check and is pushed as adjusted Mar 3 (fine). But consider a view ending Mar 1 (effectiveEnd=Mar 1): raw Mar 1 <= Mar 1 passes, emitted as Mar 3 which is beyond the requested window. Symmetrically with 'before', an occurrence on Apr 1 (Tue, just past a view ending Mar 31) is not generated even though it would not have shifted; but a Sat-Apr-1-equivalent with 'before' shifting into Mar 31 is missed because the raw Apr date fails date<=effectiveEnd. Correct: test window membership against the adjusted date, or expand the scan window by +/-3 days and then filter on the adjusted date.
- Suggested fix: Compute adjusted = adjustForWeekend(date, ...) first, then test adjusted (and/or both raw and adjusted) against [viewStartDate, effectiveEnd] before pushing. Alternatively scan a slightly widened raw window and filter the final adjusted dates against the true window.
- First-round verification: 3 votes, 0 refutes

### [F41] (medium/edge-case) Daily frequency with weekend adjustment collapses weekend days onto adjacent weekday, triple-counting amounts and colliding IDs
- File: app/lib/logic/projectionEngine/occurrenceCalculator.ts : 50-57
- Why wrong: For a daily schedule every calendar day produces an occurrence, then adjustForWeekend collapses Saturday and Sunday onto the same neighboring weekday. With 'after', both Sat and Sun map to Monday, and Monday itself also occurs, so Monday receives three occurrences while Sat/Sun receive none. The daily occurrenceId is formatDate(adjustedDate), so all three share the same Monday ID. This both visually misplaces money and (after projectionMerger's occurrenceId dedup) silently drops two of the three. Daily schedules should ignore weekend adjustment (a daily series cannot meaningfully 'skip' weekends by piling them onto Monday).
- Scenario: Daily expense $10, weekendAdjustment='after', week of 2025-01-10. Emitted/adjusted: Fri Jan10->Jan10, Sat Jan11->Jan13, Sun Jan12->Jan13, Mon Jan13->Jan13. Monday shows three $10 daily expenses (id 2025-01-13 thrice); Sat and Sun show none. Correct: daily occurrences should not be weekend-adjusted (or weekend days simply omitted, not stacked), so each day shows exactly its own $10.
- Suggested fix: Skip adjustForWeekend for the daily frequency (pass 'none'), or for daily-with-skip semantics drop weekend dates entirely rather than collapsing them onto a single weekday.
- First-round verification: 3 votes, 0 refutes
