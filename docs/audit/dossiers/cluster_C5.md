# Cluster C5

Loan form UI: contradictory monthly payments, calculationType (flat vs reducing) ignored, total-interest wrong for partially-paid loans

Source files implicated:
- app/components/pages/expenses/components/ExpenseRuleForm/components/LoanDetailsForm.tsx
- app/components/pages/expenses/components/ExpenseRuleForm/formHelpers.ts

## Member findings (from first-round analysts)

### [F19] (high/logic-error) Loan form shows two contradictory monthly payments; amortization preview amortizes the remaining balance over the FULL original term
- File: app/components/pages/expenses/components/ExpenseRuleForm/components/LoanDetailsForm.tsx : 27-54, 130-147
- Why wrong: The 'Calculated Monthly Payment' card computes the EMI from the ORIGINAL principal (loanPrincipal) over termMonths. The amortization preview table immediately below computes its own EMI internally from the CURRENT balance (loanCurrentBalance || loanPrincipal) — calculateAmortizationSchedule recomputes payment = PMT(balance, rate, termMonths) when no monthlyPayment is passed. For an existing loan where the current balance is lower than the original principal, these two numbers disagree, so the user sees one headline payment and a completely different per-row payment. Worse, the preview amortizes the remaining balance over the FULL original termMonths rather than the remaining term, so the per-row payment is understated. The correct behavior is to amortize the remaining balance over the remaining term (term minus payments already made), and the headline EMI and the schedule must use the same balance/term.
- Scenario: Original principal 10,000, current balance 5,000, 6% APR, 12-month term. The 'Calculated Monthly Payment' card shows 860.66 (PMT on 10,000/12mo). The amortization preview rows show 430.33 (PMT on 5,000/12mo). Both are presented at once and both are wrong for a half-paid loan: the real remaining payment on 5,000 over the ~6 remaining months would be ~847, not 430.33 and not 860.66.
- Suggested fix: Drive both the headline EMI and the preview from the same inputs. Compute the schedule from the balance actually being financed (currentBalance for an existing loan, principal for a new one) and over the REMAINING term, and display calculatedPayment = schedule[0].payment so the card and the table never diverge.

### [F20] (high/correctness) Selected loan calculationType (flat_rate / reducing_balance) is ignored: EMI and total interest are always amortized
- File: app/components/pages/expenses/components/ExpenseRuleForm/formHelpers.ts : 262-274
- Why wrong: The form exposes a Calculation Type selector with three options — amortized, reducing_balance, and flat_rate (see LoanDetailsForm FormSelect bound to loanCalculationType and LOAN_CALCULATION_TYPES in constants.ts) — and stores loanConfig.calculationType. But calculateLoanPayment ignores the type entirely and always applies the standard amortizing PMT formula. The 'Calculated Monthly Payment', the 'Total Interest', and the amortization preview are therefore wrong for any loan that is not 'amortized'. A flat-rate loan charges interest on the original principal for the whole term (interest = principal x rate x years; payment = (principal+interest)/months), which is materially higher than the amortized payment. The stored calculationType is never read by the projection engine either (grep of app/lib finds no consumer), so the feature is non-functional, but at minimum the form must not display amortized numbers when the user picked flat_rate/reducing_balance.
- Scenario: Principal 12,000, 10% rate, 24 months, calculationType = 'flat_rate'. The form shows monthly payment 553.74 and total interest 1,289.74 (amortized). The correct flat-rate figures are payment 600.00 and total interest 2,400.00 — the user is shown roughly half the true interest cost.
- Suggested fix: Pass loanCalculationType into calculateLoanPayment and branch: flat_rate -> (principal*(1 + (annualRate/100)*(termMonths/12)))/termMonths; reducing_balance -> standard amortized PMT (interest on remaining balance, same as 'amortized'); amortized -> current formula. Apply the same branching to the total-interest display and the schedule, and have the projection engine honor calculationType.

### [F21] (medium/correctness) Loan 'Total Interest' metric is wrong for existing (partially-paid) loans
- File: app/components/pages/expenses/components/ExpenseRuleForm/components/LoanDetailsForm.tsx : 139-145
- Why wrong: Total interest is computed as calculatedPayment * termMonths - principal. calculatedPayment here is the EMI on the full original principal over the full term, so for a brand-new loan this is the lifetime interest and is fine. But when the user enters a Current Balance lower than the principal (an existing loan), this formula still multiplies the full-principal EMI by the full term and subtracts the full principal, which has no relationship to the interest that will actually accrue. The projection engine (loanProjections.ts) actually amortizes loanConfig.currentBalance over termMonths - paymentsMade, so the real remaining interest is interest on the current balance over the remaining term. The displayed figure overstates remaining interest because it includes interest on principal already repaid. The same flawed expression is duplicated in ReviewStep.tsx lines 168-171.
- Scenario: Principal 20,000, current balance 8,000, 8% APR, 36-month term. Displayed total interest = EMI(20000,8%,36)*36 - 20000 = 2,565.44. The interest that will actually be charged going forward is the interest on the remaining 8,000 balance over the remaining term, far less than 2,565. The metric misrepresents the cost of the existing loan.
- Suggested fix: Base total interest on what will actually be paid: sum the interest column of the schedule built from the financed balance over the remaining term (schedule.reduce((s, r) => s + r.interest, 0)), or for a new loan use EMI*term - currentBalance with the same balance/term used for the EMI. Fix both LoanDetailsForm and ReviewStep.
